# Cold email to unwarmed ICP contacts books at least 2 discovery calls per 40 sent

Opened: 2026-06-08

Serves: B1

Status: closed

## Hypothesis

Cold email to ICP-matched contacts with no prior relationship books discovery
calls at a rate that pays for the hours it costs. Of 40 messages sent to
unwarmed contacts, at least 2 result in a booked discovery call.

Ranked cheapest to test by the assumption audit on 2026-06-08: the drafts and
the list already existed, so the only new cost was the founder's sending time.

## Threshold

2 booked discovery calls per 40 messages sent, by 2026-07-10.

Below 2 means the channel does not pay for itself at this list size, and the
hours belong to the warm network instead.

## Judgment

Judgment date: 2026-07-10

Source: pipeline.md ## Live and ## Dead — stage basis lines record how each
prospect arrived, and drafts/outreach/ ## Sent records what the founder actually
sent.

## Result

0 booked discovery calls from 41 messages sent, read 2026-07-10 from
pipeline.md ## Live and ## Dead. Two replies, both declining. Every live deal in
the pipeline on that date arrived through the warm network.

The 41 messages cost 9 founder hours including list building.

## Verdict

not met — cold email stops. The 9 hours a month move to the warm network, where
both current live deals originated.

Handed to: decision-log — the channel is being dropped and the hours reallocated,
which is not cheap to take back once the list goes stale.
