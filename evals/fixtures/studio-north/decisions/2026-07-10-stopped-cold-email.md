# Stopped cold outreach and moved the hours to the warm network

Date: 2026-07-10

Decided by: founder

Serves: B1

## Context

The experiment opened on 2026-06-08 set a threshold of 2 booked discovery calls
per 40 cold messages, judged 2026-07-10. It returned 0 from 41 messages at a
cost of 9 founder hours, and both replies declined
([[2026-06-08-cold-email-books-discovery-calls]]). Every live deal on that date
arrived through the warm network.

The threshold was written on 2026-06-08, before any message went out. That is
what makes this a result rather than a preference.

## Rejected

Send another 40 with a rewritten opener. It lost because the experiment was
scoped to test the channel, not the copy, and a second run at the same cost
would have been the first extension of a judgment date rather than a new test.
If the copy is the real hypothesis it deserves its own file and its own
threshold.

## What would change our mind

An inbound reply from an unwarmed contact who names the sequence, or 3 or more
qualified replies from any single warm-network introduction batch failing to
appear by 2026-09-30 — which would mean the warm network is also not producing
and cold was not the weak channel.

None.
